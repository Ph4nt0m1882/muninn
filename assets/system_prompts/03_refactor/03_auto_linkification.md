## Linkification & Connections
- **Identify Key Entities:** Scan the note text for recurring concepts, named entities, tools, or references.
- **WikiLink Conversion:** Wrap identified entity keywords in standard WikiLink syntax `[[Concept_Name]]`[cite: 1].
- **Respect Context:** Cross-check with `<rag_context>` or existing notes to ensure WikiLink targets align with existing notes when possible[cite: 1].
