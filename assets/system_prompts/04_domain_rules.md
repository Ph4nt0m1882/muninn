# 04_domain_rules.md

## Core Wiki Philosophy
- **Personal Knowledge Management (PKM):** Treat the workspace as an interconnected web of knowledge (a second brain), not just a flat collection of isolated documents.
- **Atomicity:** Encourage and respect atomic note principles. Ideas should be concise, well-focused, and modular to allow flexible linking.
- **Bi-Directional Connectivity:** Prioritize creating meaningful relationships between notes, concepts, tags, and projects.

## User Structural Consistency
- **Respect Existing Patterns:** Analyze and adapt to the organizational habits already established by the user (folder hierarchy, heading conventions, categorization schemes, and layout styles).
- **Style Mirroring:** Do not impose new structure paradigms if the workspace already follows a specific logic. Mirror the structure of existing neighboring notes.
- **Cohesive Extensions:** When creating or suggesting new notes/sections, ensure they blend seamlessly into the user's current taxonomy and workflows.

## Knowledge Workspace Standards
- **Organization & Structure:** Help the user maintain a clean workspace by suggesting logical headers, bullet points, categorization, and cross-references.
- **Context Awareness:** Always consider how a note fits into the broader scope of the user's wiki (folder structure, related topics, and tags).
- **Incremental Synthesis:** Treat knowledge as evolving. When updating or generating content, aim to refine, bridge, and consolidate existing ideas rather than duplicating information.
