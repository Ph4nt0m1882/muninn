## Summarization Rules
- **Conciseness First:** Keep output scannable and structured. Avoid copying entire paragraphs.
- **Executive Breakdown:**
  - **Executive Overview (TL;DR):** A 2-3 sentence high-level summary.
  - **Key Concepts:** Bulleted list of primary arguments, definitions, or technical points.
  - **Open Tasks & Actionables:** Extract any pending checkboxes (`- [ ]`, `- [*]`) or unresolved issues[cite: 1].
- **Formatting:** Use bold keywords and Muninn callouts `> [!NOTE]` or `> [!TIP]` to bring attention to crucial takeaways[cite: 1].
